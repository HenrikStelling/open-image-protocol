# Internal thoracic width from CXAS rib masks, and what it says about the CTR denominator (2026-10-17)

Question (PLAN Phase 3b, "CTR truth Tier 1 → Tier 2"): the converter's CTR divides the heart width by the width of the union of
both lung masks. Does that lung-union width under-estimate the internal thoracic width (inner rib margins), and would a rib-based
denominator change the ratio or its clinical separation?

## Method
- CXAS (UNet-ResNet50, 159 structures, 512-px model, masks restored to the original size) run on the 100 VinDr paper packages
  (`scripts/cxas_worker.py`, masks in `<pkg>/derived/masks_cxas/`: union of all ribs, heart, both lungs; 2.5 s per image).
- Two conventions for the inner margin, both per image row within the lung-bearing rows, then the maximum over rows:
  - **v2, end of run:** the lateral-most rib run on each side marks the outer chest wall; its inner end is the inner margin; rows
    where that run is longer than 3 % of the image width (an oblique posterior rib merged with the lateral segment) are skipped.
    Fails on images where every row is skipped (5 of 100).
  - **q25, outer edge minus thickness:** inner margin = outer edge of the lateral-most run minus the 25th percentile of the
    lateral run lengths over all rows (the tangential rib cortex); never fails.
  - The September function (`anatomy_cxas.internal_thoracic_width_px`, "walk inward along the contiguous run") followed oblique
    ribs into the lung field and produced widths down to 74 % of the lung union (CTR 0.74 on one pilot image); it is replaced.
- Reference for the ratio itself: the VinDr radiologist label "Cardiomegaly" (22 of the 100 images), AUC of each CTR variant.
- Heart width three ways: PSPNet (package), CXAS, CheXmask (HybridGNet, `bench/results/_chexmask/vindr-paper.json`).

## Readings
1. **The lung-union width is not too narrow.** The inner rib margin is 1.5 % (v2) to 3.4 % (q25) *narrower* than the lung union,
   i.e. within the resolution of a 512-px rib mask (one tangential rib cortex is 50–120 px on a 2,700-px image, 2–4 % per side).
   A rib-based denominator would raise the CTR slightly, not lower it. The September hypothesis that the lung-union thorax
   inflates the CTR is refuted; the "definition problem" noted in the CheXmask readings is withdrawn.
2. **The package heart width is corroborated by CXAS** (1 % apart); CheXmask's HybridGNet heart is the wide one (+3 %). Two of
   three automatic sources agree with the converter.
3. **The package CTR is the best separator of the radiologist label** (AUC 0.949; 90 % accuracy at 0.52). Neither the rib
   denominator nor the other heart sources improve it. 12 of 78 unlabelled images exceed 0.50 (15 %): borderline cases and
   label noise, not a systematic bias.
4. **The high share of CTR > 0.5 in these sets reflects the population**: 22 % of the paper images carry the cardiomegaly
   label, and VinDr is a hospital series with many abnormal studies; the ~18 % "expected prevalence" quoted earlier was a
   population figure that does not apply here.
5. **Consequences.** (a) The converter keeps the lung-union definition; the measurement's `method` text and the spec gain the
   sentence that the lung-union width equals the inner rib margin within 2–3 % on 100 CXAS-segmented images. (b)
   `anatomy_cxas.internal_thoracic_width_px` is replaced by the q25 convention (always defined) with v2 as the reported
   alternative; it stays an optional second source, not a package default. (c) The CheXmask cross-check records stand as
   written (segmentation agreement); their readings and PLAN are corrected. (d) `validation_status` remains `cross_checked`:
   the label AUC is a clinical plausibility check, not a reference measurement.
