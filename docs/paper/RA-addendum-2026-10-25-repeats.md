# RA addendum 2026-10-25: repeat subset (run-to-run reliability)

For the Research_Automation session. Source of every number: `docs/reports/repeat-subset-2026-10-24.md`, generated by
`bench/repeat_report.py` from the five run directories labelled `b7:repeat_subset` (regenerated 2026-11-02 with all ten models). This answers the
co-author's point A6.3 ("one reply per cell in the paper run") with data instead of a disclaimer.

## What was run

- 20 VinDr-CXR paper packages (seed-0 shuffle of `vindr-paper`, a subset of the paper's 100), three replies per item.
- Conditions `raw`, `ctx` (full file), `misled_plain`, `misled_oip`; tasks left edge, scale, CTR, cardiac width, flip check,
  wrong-label adoption. 136 items per repeat (76 in `raw`, which has no flip check), 696 replies per model.
- Seven Ollama models on 2026-10-24 (gemma4:31b, glm-5.3-flash, kimi-k3, minimax-m3, mistral-large-3 in the cloud;
  gemma4:e4b-it-qat and medgemma1.5:4b locally); 4,872 replies, 0 errors. claude (claude-sonnet-5) on 2026-10-31: 696
  replies, 0 errors; every full-file cell, the flip check (48 %) and plain-note adoption identical across the three repeats,
  largest spread image-only cardiac width 44–56 %. gpt (gpt-5.6-terra) and gemini (gemini-3.8-flash) on 2026-11-02: 696
  replies each, 0 errors; both identical on every full-file cell; gpt image-only CTR 15–35 %, gemini 50–60 %; flip check
  gpt 88 % and gemini 98 % in every repeat (the two models above chance). All ten models: 6,960 replies.
- Caveat: since 2026-10-17 the packages carry the CheXmask cross-check table and template 0.2.1, so the full-file prompt is
  not byte-identical to the paper run's. Within the repeat run every item received the same prompt in all three repeats
  (0 items with more than one prompt hash).

## Findings (110 cells)

| Cell family | Run-to-run behaviour |
|---|---|
| Full file: left edge, scale, CTR, cardiac width | same verdict on every item in every repeat for 8 of 10 models (claude, gpt, gemini included); glm varied on CTR (70–95 %) and cardiac width (75–94 %), medgemma on the left edge (65–85 %) |
| Flip check | the six models that always agree (claude included): same verdict on 90–100 % of items, 50 % in every repeat; glm 72–85 %, kimi 48–62 %; gpt 88 % and gemini 98 % in every repeat (above chance, 82 % and 100 % of items stable) |
| Image only: CTR, cardiac width | range 10–25 pp between repeats for 6 of 10 models (gpt 12–20; mistral 0, medgemma 6–10, claude 5–12, gemini 6–10); 25–75 % of items keep their verdict, i.e. 3–4 of 16–20 items move |
| Wrong-label adoption | stable (≥ 85 % of items) for gemma4:31b, mistral, medgemma, gemini; gpt 75–80 % of items stable (misled_oip 35–50 %); kimi misled_oip 75 → 60 → 40 %, gemma4:e4b 35–50 %, minimax 40–55 % of items stable |
| Paper reply vs. the three repeats | outside the repeat range in 16 of 77 Ollama cells (claude 1, gpt 1, gemini 3 of 11, each within 6 pp), by more than 10 pp in 6: image-only size or scale cells (glm cardiac width, kimi CTR, minimax CTR, gemma4:e4b scale) and gemma4:e4b's wrong-label cells (misled_oip paper 80 % vs. 35–50 % now) |

## Suggested manuscript changes

1. **Methods, Post-hoc experiments** (after the conflict-trial sentence): "On a fixed subset of 20 VinDr-CXR images the
   four main conditions were repeated three times for all ten models (6,960 replies, 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-02;
   Supplementary Table S17)."
2. **Limitations**, replace "In the paper run each question was answered once per model and condition, so run-to-run
   reliability was measured only in the conflict trials." with: "In the paper run each question was answered once per model
   and condition. A three-repeat re-run of the main conditions on 20 images (all ten models) gave identical verdicts on
   every item for the full-file side, scale and size questions in eight of ten models and for the flip check of the models
   at chance, whereas image-only size estimates and wrong-label adoption changed verdict on 3–4 of 20 images between runs
   (Supplementary Table S17); the single-reply cells of those two kinds should be read with that spread."
3. **Supplementary Table S17** (new): the per-model overview of `repeat-subset-2026-10-24.md` (cells, largest range, median
   share of stable items, median kappa), plus one sentence that kappa is near zero for near-constant verdicts and that the
   stable share is the primary measure. Optionally the full per-cell table as S17b.
4. **Supplementary Methods S4** (conflict trials) gains a cross-reference: the identical-verdict shares there and in S17 are
   computed the same way.
5. gpt and gemini are now included (2026-11-02), so S17 and the Limitations sentence cover all ten models. Note for
   S17: for the wrong-label cells the paper run drew a different wrong label per image, so only the adoption rates, not the
   per-item agreement, are comparable with the paper.
